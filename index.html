About Me

I am a passionate video editor with 1 year of professional experience in post-production. My journey began with a simple love for storytelling, and today I specialize in transforming raw footage into compelling narratives.

I believe that editing is more than just cutting clips it's about controlling the rhythm, emotion, and pace of the story. Whether it's a high-energy promo for a fashion brand or a deep-dive interview, I focus on creating a seamless flow that keeps the viewer engaged from the first second to the last.

When I'm not editing, you can find me exploring new cinematic techniques, studying color theory, or hiking to find inspiration in nature.
